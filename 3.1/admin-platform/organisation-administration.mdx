---
title: "Safe Share Organization Administration"
---

*Safe Share Organization Administration* is a web-based interface for administering an organization's access to resources on a Covata Platform instance. This interface also provides access to a number of configurable features of Covata technologies <sup>&#42;</sup> that apply to the organization *and* Covata users who are members of the organization.

![Organization Administration dashboard page](/images/4ffdb2d5a9e5-organisation-administration-dashboard-page.png)
*Organization administrators* are users who, once [signed in](/3.1/admin-platform/accessing-organisation-administration#signing-in-to-organisation-administration) to Safe Share Organization Administration, can access its [dashboard](/3.1/admin-platform/accessing-organisation-administration#the-organisation-administration-dashboard) (depicted above) and have the ability to perform the following administration activities within their organization on their Covata Platform instance:

- Configure [users](/3.1/admin-platform/administering-users-within-an-organisation) (also known as 'Covata users') who are members of their organization by initially adding new or pre-registered user accounts on the Covata Platform.
- Configure and manage their organization's [user whitelist](/3.1/admin-platform/managing-user-whitelists), defining which people can be shared items within the organization, including people who are not organization members.
- Manage [classifications](/3.1/admin-platform/managing-classifications) and [groups](/3.1/admin-platform/managing-groups), both of which are used in conjunction to help restrict the people with whom content can be shared.
- Manage [plans](/3.1/admin-platform/managing-users-storage-quotas-through-plans) that define storage quotas, which define the amount of storage (ultimately managed by the Covata Platform's Content Service) available to individual Covata user accounts.
- Disable or re-enable access to any [files](/3.1/admin-platform/administering-files) encrypted using Covata technologies on the Covata Platform, as well as view all recorded activities on these files.
- Generate [reports](/3.1/admin-platform/generating-reports) on requests made by client applications to the Covata Platform's API.
- Change the [configuration](/3.1/admin-platform/configuring-organisation-administration-properties) of Safe Share Organization Administration properties.

<sup>&#42;</sup> *Covata technologies* refers to all features and functionality provided by (and made available through) the Covata Platform, including Covata's Safe Share applications, the Covata Platform's API and the Covata Platform itself.
