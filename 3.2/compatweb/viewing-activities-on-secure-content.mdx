---
title: "Viewing activities on secure content"
---

Users of the Safe Share web application can view a history of [specific activities](/3.2/compatweb/viewing-activities-on-secure-content#types-of-activities-indicated) performed on a file or folder.

**To view the activities performed on a file or folder:**

1. [Sign in](/3.2/compatweb/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. [Locate](/3.2/compatweb/managing-secure-content#finding-secure-content) the file or folder whose activity you wish to view.
3. *Highlight* the item, by clicking on the item's row (to the right of its name): <br />
   ![Highlighting a shared item as an owner](/images/2c7d43465919-highlighting-a-shared-item-owner.png)
4. Click the **History** tab to reveal a history of activities performed on the item in reverse chronological order: <br />
   ![Activity feed as an owner](/images/c39db0fa0de8-activity-feed-owner.png)

## Types of activities indicated

The types of activities indicated on a file or folder (i.e. item) depend on the following conditions:

- As one of the following:

  - the *owner* of an item,
  - a *collaborator*<sup>1</sup> with **Manage** [permissions](/3.2/compatweb/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-a-permission-set) on an item, or
  - a *collaborator* with **Upload** permissions on an item you created<sup>2</sup>,

  the **History** tab shows when (and by whom) the item was created, as well as each time anyone with access to the item:

  - [Viewed](/3.2/compatweb/accessing-secure-content#viewing-and-printing-secure-content) or [downloaded](/3.2/compatweb/accessing-secure-content#downloading-secure-content) the item (if it is a file).
  - Modified:

    - the item's name or
    - the item's location.
  - As the owner of the item only, modified:

    - whom the item is [shared](/3.2/compatweb/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with or
    - a [collaborator's permissions](/3.2/compatweb/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-a-permission-set) on the item (when [sharing](/3.2/compatweb/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) it).
- As a *collaborator* with either **Download** or **View** permissions on a file you *did not* create, the **History** tab shows each time you either [viewed](/3.2/compatweb/accessing-secure-content#viewing-and-printing-secure-content) or [downloaded](/3.2/compatweb/accessing-secure-content#downloading-secure-content) that file.
- As a *collaborator* with **Upload**, **Download** or **View** permissions on a folder you *did not* create, no activity is shown on the **History** tab.

<sup>1</sup> A *collaborator* on an item is someone who has permission to access the item. However, the collaborator does not own this item because the item is located in a folder owned by someone else. <br />
<sup>2</sup> A *collaborator* in this context is yourself, having created a folder or uploaded a file within a folder owned by someone else. If the item is a file, then Safe Share implicitly downgrades its permissions from **Upload** to **Download** since, from a usability perspective, it is not possible to upload content to a file.
