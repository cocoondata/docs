## What is a Microsoft Add-in? <a id="what-is-a-ms-addin" />

A Microsoft (MS) Add-in allows you to extend your web application functionality into the Office environment, thereby allowing users to access your web application functions without having to leave the Office tool they are in. Think of it as a “lightweight” browser within your Office tools.

## Benefits of a SafeShare Microsoft Add-in <a id="benefits-of-a-safeshare-ms-addin" />

- Reduces the likelihood of accidental sharing of files within Outlook.
- Allows users to revoke access to files in real-time.
- Full audit trail of all actions performed by all authorised users on a file.
- Manage your SafeShare data from within the MS productivity tools.
