---
title: "Using the Microsoft Office Add-in"
---

1. Search for SafeShare for Office and Install the add-in from the Microsoft Store by following the steps mentioned under [How to install a Microsoft Add-in?](/CS1.2/ms-addins/microsoft-add-ins-overview#how-to-install-a-ms-addin). Once installed, it should appear on the ribbon bar.

   ![SafeShare for Office Add-in](/images/8ba7d9affaf7-office-addin-on-ribbon-bar.png)

   <br />
2. Click on the icon and the add-in should open as below:

   ![SafeShare Office Add-in Login Page](/images/4cb638f20998-office-addin-login-page.png)

   <br />
3. Check the “Service address setup” link and make sure this points to your unique instance id of SafeShare as below:

   ![SafeShare Service Address Setup](/images/20151c6d7c1d-service-address-setup.png)

   <br />
4. Enter your SafeShare login details and click “Sign In”. If you have Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enabled, you will be presented with the below screen:

   ![SafeShare MFA enabled](/images/ac1f5bd58325-mfa-enabled.png)

   <br />
5. Enter your MFA details and click “Authenticate” and you should be taken to the Homepage:

   ![SafeShare Office Add-in Home Page](/images/46bbcfc462a2-homepage-after-successful-login.png)

   <br />
6. Click on "Secure" to securely upload the Word, Excel or PowerPoint document that you have open.
7. Pick a location within SafeShare, give it a file name and click the “Secure” button to securely upload the document as below:

   ![Secure files with Office Add-in](/images/a12327d8fd46-securing-files-with-office-addin.png)

   <br />
8. Click OK once the file has been securely uploaded as below:

   ![File Secured Successfully](/images/6d13030dfe95-file-secured-successfully.png)

   <br />
9. You will then be taken back to the Homepage. Click on "Open" to browse to your existing secured documents as below:

   ![SafeShare Office Add-in Home Page](/images/dc4486c15afb-homepage-after-successful-upload.png)

   <br />
10. You should see all your folders/files, including the newly secured file/s you just uploaded. If you highlight a file you can either click on the 3 dots (…) or right-click on the file to see further options as below:

    ![Right-Click a file for Options](/images/e0d3cf6d06a9-right-click-a-file-for-options.png)

    <br />
11. If you click on “View”, a browser window will open and show the file.<sup>&#42;</sup>
12. If you click “Download”, a browser window will open and download the file.<sup>&#42;</sup>
13. If you click “Edit in Office Online”, a browser window will open and you will be able to edit the file with Microsoft Online.<sup>&#42;</sup>

    <sup>&#42;</sup>*Please note that you will be prompted to login again if there is no active browser session for SafeShare.*
14. If you click on "Share", you will be able to edit the sharing permissions for a file as below:

    ![Sharing a file using the Office Add-in](/images/3195802f3afa-sharing-a-file-using-office-addin.png)

    <br />
15. You can do everything you would normally do in the browser version of SafeShare such as: add Collaborators with individual sharing permissions, set the classification of the file and add a sharing period.
16. When you are done, click “Share” and the permissions will be updated, and you will be taken back to your Homepage.
17. For Settings, click on the Settings wheel in the top right as below:

    ![Office Add-in Settings](/images/4e3e72646cd0-office-addin-settings.png)

    <br />
18. Here you will be able to switch between different organisations that you may belong to:

    ![Switch Organisations](/images/6733b8b03272-switch-organisations.png)

    <br />
19. And “Sign out” as below:

    ![Sign-Out from SafeShare Office Add-in](/images/219d0372d32f-signout-from-office-addin.png)

    <br />
20. Lastly, on the Homepage there are two links at the bottom, one for support which will open a browser window and take you to our support portal, and a legal link which opens a browser window and takes you to our licence agreement.

    ![Leal Document for SafeShare Office Add-in](/images/68f5ac9e0eb7-legal-document-link.png)
