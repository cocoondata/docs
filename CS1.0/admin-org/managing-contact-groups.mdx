---
title: "Managing contact groups"
---

The **Contact groups** page of Organisation Administration ([below](/CS1.0/admin-org/managing-contact-groups#contact-groups-page)) allows the following within an organisation:

- The [addition of new contact groups](/CS1.0/admin-org/managing-contact-groups#adding-a-new-contact-group).
- Existing contact groups to be [edited](/CS1.0/admin-org/managing-contact-groups#editing-an-existing-contact-group) or [removed](/CS1.0/admin-org/managing-contact-groups#removing-an-existing-contact-group).

Organisation administrators can create and modify *contact groups*, each of which is a group of Covata users (with the [Originator role](/CS1.0/admin-org/administering-users-within-an-organisation#originator-role)) with whom items can be shared. This avoids the need for a Covata user to specify each email address of a group of users each time they need to share an item with that group of users.

## Adding a new contact group

1. Ensure you are [signed in](/CS1.0/admin-org/accessing-organisation-administration#signing-in-to-organisation-administration) to Organisation Administration.
2. Click the **Contact groups** option on the left of the [Organisation Administration interface](/CS1.0/admin-org/accessing-organisation-administration#the-organisation-administration-interface) to open the **Contact groups** page.
3. Click the **Create** button.
4. In the **New contact group** dialog box, specify the:

   - **Name** - A name that best describes the contact group's members. This is the name of the contact group that Covata users would specify when sharing an item with this contact group.
   - **Description ( *Optional* )** - A longer description to provide more information about the contact group.
   - **Manage conctacts** - In this section, any Covata users (with the [Originator role](/CS1.0/admin-org/administering-users-within-an-organisation#originator-role)) whose email addresses appear in the **Selected users** section on the right will be members of the contact group:

     | To do the following: | Do this: | Which results in this: |
     | --- | --- | --- |
     | Add a user to the contact group | Click the email address of that user in the **Available users** section. | Transfers that user to the **Selected users** section. |
     | Remove a user from the contact group | Click the email address of that user in the **Selected users** section. | Transfers that user to the **Available users** section. |
     | Find specific users by filtering them (making it easier to add or remove them above) | In either the **Available users** or **Selected users** sections, begin typing (into its respective **Filter** box) a series of characters contained in the Covata users' email addresses. | The list of users (in their respective section/s) being filtered to those whose email address contains the subset of characters entered into its **Filter** box. |
     | Add all users to the contact group | Click the  ![Add all button](/images/ba0414469515-add-all.png) <br />  (Add all) button in the **Available users** section. | Transfers all remaining users in this section to **Selected users**. |
     | Remove all users from the contact group | Click the  ![Remove all button](/images/ceb2b8442518-remove-all.png) <br />  (Remove all) button in the **Selected users** section. | Transfers all remaining users in this section to **Available users**. |

## Editing an existing contact group

1. Ensure you are [signed in](/CS1.0/admin-org/accessing-organisation-administration#signing-in-to-organisation-administration) to Organisation Administration.
2. Click the **Contact groups** option on the left of the [Organisation Administration interface](/CS1.0/admin-org/accessing-organisation-administration#the-organisation-administration-interface) to open the **Contact groups** page.
3. Locate/scroll to the relevant contact group and click its row.
4. Click the **Edit** button.
5. In the **Edit contact group** dialog box, modify the:

   - **Name** - A name that best describes the contact group's members. This is the name of the contact group that Covata users would specify when sharing an item with this contact group.
   - **Description ( *Optional* )** - A longer description to provide more information about the contact group.
   - **Manage conctacts** - In this section, any Covata users (with the [Originator role](/CS1.0/admin-org/administering-users-within-an-organisation#originator-role)) whose email addresses appear in the **Selected users** section on the right will be members of the contact group:

     | To do the following: | Do this: | Which results in this: |
     | --- | --- | --- |
     | Add a user to the contact group | Click the email address of that user in the **Available users** section. | Transfers that user to the **Selected users** section. |
     | Remove a user from the contact group | Click the email address of that user in the **Selected users** section. | Transfers that user to the **Available users** section. |
     | Find specific users by filtering them (making it easier to add or remove them above) | In either the **Available users** or **Selected users** sections, begin typing (into its respective **Filter** box) a series of characters contained in the Covata users' email addresses. | The list of users (in their respective section/s) being filtered to those whose email address contains the subset of characters entered into its **Filter** box. |
     | Add all users to the contact group | Click the  ![Add all button](/images/ba0414469515-add-all.png) <br />  (Add all) button in the **Available users** section. | Transfers all remaining users in this section to **Selected users**. |

## Removing an existing contact group

1. Ensure you are [signed in](/CS1.0/admin-org/accessing-organisation-administration#signing-in-to-organisation-administration) to Organisation Administration.
2. Click the **Contact groups** option on the left of the [Organisation Administration interface](/CS1.0/admin-org/accessing-organisation-administration#the-organisation-administration-interface) to open the **Contact groups** page.
3. Locate/scroll to the relevant contact group and click its row.
4. Click the **Remove** button.
5. On the **Remove contact group** message box, click **Yes** to proceed with the contact group's removal.
