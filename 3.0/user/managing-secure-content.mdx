## Finding secure content

The Safe Share web application provides the following mechanisms for locating files and folders within your organization:

- [Searching for (a) specific item/s](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#searching-for-items) by name or by the owner who shared the item/s with you and/or
- [Filtering items](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#filtering-items) using the item filters at the top-left of the main Safe Share page.

Although these features work with items in your current organization only, they become useful as increasingly more items are added (either by yourself or shared with you by other people) within your organization/s.

<a id="searching-for-items" />**To search for (a) specific item/s:**

1. [Sign in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. If the Home icon at the top-left indicates the word 'Home' and no drop-down icon is indicated, skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#beginning-your-search). (This indicates that you are a member of only one organization.) <br />
   *Otherwise*, the name of your current organization is indicated, from which you can either:

   - skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#beginning-your-search) to search an item from this organization, or
   - search an item from a different organization (you are a member of) by [changing your organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) before continuing on.
3. <a id="beginning-your-search" />In the search box at the top of the main Safe Share page,

   ![Search box](/images/0fd04e28912f-search-box.png)

   <br />
    begin typing a series of characters contained in any of these item's fields:

   - **Name**
   - **Owner**'s email address
   - **Owner**'s first or last name
4. Click the search (i.e. magnifying glass) icon to the right of the search box (or type the 'Enter/Return' key) and the list of items in your organization is filtered to those whose name, owner's email address or owner's first/last name contains the subset of characters entered in the search box. <br />
   **Tips:**

   - To clear your search results, click the **Clear Search** link at the top of the main Safe Share page.
   - To clear the search box, click the **x** icon that appears after typing one or more characters.
   - You can also sort items on the main Safe Share page by clicking the name (in the column header) of the relevant field by which you would like to sort your items. Clicking a field name multiple times rotates through sorting the items (according to that field) in ascending, descending, followed by natural order (which is ascending order by the item's **Name** field).

<a id="filtering-items" />**To filter items:**

1. [Sign in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. If the Home icon at the top-left indicates the word 'Home' and no drop-down icon is indicated, skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#beginning-your-filtering). (This indicates that you are a member of only one organization.) <br />
   *Otherwise*, the name of your current organization is indicated, from which you can either:

   - skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#beginning-your-filtering) to filter items from this organization, or
   - filter items from a different organization (you are a member of) by [changing your organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) before continuing on.
3. <a id="beginning-your-filtering" />At the top-left of the main Safe Share page, click the **All files** drop-down and choose:

   - **My files** to filter for all items (at/from the root level) that you own within your organization,
   - **Shared with me** to filter for all [items that other people have shared with you](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) as a collaborator or
   - **Sharing** to filter for all items within your organization that you have shared with other people.

   Choose the default **All files** option to show all items within your organization you can access from the root level.

<Note>

If your user account does not have the **Originator** role for your (current) organization, then the **All files** (and other filters) drop-down will not be available because you do not have your own storage space to [upload, securely store](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-and-storing-personally-owned-content) and [share](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) personally owned content within this organization.

</Note>

## Organizing content with folders <a id="organising-content-with-folders" />

To help organize files within your organization in storage (managed by the Covata Platform), you can create folders, which also facilitates the ability to [share multiple items of your securely stored content](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with a group of people.

**To create a new folder:**

1. [Sign in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. If the Home icon at the top-left indicates the word 'Home' and no drop-down icon is indicated, skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#navigating-to-a-subfolder). (This indicates that you are a member of only one organization.) <br />
   *Otherwise*, the name of your current organization is indicated, from which you can either:

   - skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#navigating-to-a-subfolder) to create a folder within this organization, or
   - create a folder within a different organization (you are a member of) by [changing your organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) before continuing on.
3. <a id="navigating-to-a-subfolder" />If you intend to create a subfolder from the root level, then from the main Safe Share page, click through to the folder that will become the parent of the subfolder you wish to create. <br />
    This parent folder could be a folder that you own, or a folder owned by another Covata user (who has granted you the **Manage** permissions as a collaborator on this folder). Once you have clicked through one or more folders, a breadcrumb (e.g.
   ![Breadcrumb](/images/c9f3a09ab094-breadcrumb.png)

    ) appears near the top, from which you can click the:

   - Ellipsis symbol ('...') to expand the entire folder hierarchy (i.e. to reveal **Shared Folder** in the example above), since only the root and current (i.e. **Other** above) folder locations are indicated after each change in folder location.
   - Link of a parent folder's name (i.e. **Shared Folder** from **Other** above) to go to that folder's location.
   - The Home icon or its adjacent **Home** link (or the linked name of your current organization) to go to the root location of your file area.
4. Click the top-left **Folder** button.
5. In the **Create new folder** dialog box, specify the name of the folder and click **Save** to create the folder. The folder is created in the current list of items area within your organization.

**To add content to a folder:**

1. Ensure you are [signed in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application, are in the [appropriate organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) and if necessary, click through to the folder to add content to.
2. Do either of the following:

   - If the folder that you own is visible in the list of items area, drag one or more file/s over the folder to upload the file/s to it in storage. The folder highlights a different color to indicate that the file/s will instead be uploaded to that folder instead of the file area's current location. <br />
     **Tip:** You can also do this with a subfolder you are a collaborator on, whose owner has granted you either **Upload** or **Manage** permissions on the parent folder.
   - If the list of items area displays the contents of the current folder, then drag the file/s onto this area. A message appears at the top, indicating that the file/s is/are ready to upload.

   For more information about uploading content to your list of items, see [step 3](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-and-dragging-files) of [Uploading and storing personally owned content](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-and-storing-personally-owned-content), as well as [Uploading content to share through another user's folder](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-content-to-share-through-another-users-folder).

<a id="folder-tips" />

<Tip>

- If a [folder](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#organising-content-with-folders) was created by someone else who has not granted you (as a collaborator on this folder) either the **Upload** or **Manage** [permissions](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-a-permission-set) (via the ['Share' dialog box](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#share-dialog-box)), then you will not be able to upload any files to that folder. See [Sharing secure content](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) and [Uploading content to share through another user's folder](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-content-to-share-through-another-users-folder) for more information.
- You can filter your list of files and folders in the list of items area to show only *your* items that you have uploaded and securely stored by choosing the **My files** [filter](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#filtering-items) (from the **All files** drop-down) at the top-left of the main Safe Share page.

</Tip>

## Unsharing secure content

'Unsharing' an *item that you own* effectively removes all or specific collaborators from the item and its contents, so that only you as the owner of this item can control who can access it.

Using Safe Share's **Unshare** feature on a folder removes all collaborators from not only this folder, but also all collaborators from every item contained within this folder (which had been set *explicitly* on these items). For more information about *explicit* versus *implicit* sharing, refer to the **Note** at the end of [step 6](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-a-permission-set) on selecting permissions for a collaborator (of the [Sharing secure content](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) procedure).

**To unshare your securely stored content:**

1. [Sign in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. If the Home icon at the top-left indicates the word 'Home' and no drop-down icon is indicated, skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-your-item-for-unsharing). (This indicates that you are a member of only one organization.) <br />
   *Otherwise*, the name of your current organization is indicated, from which you can either:

   - skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-your-item-for-unsharing) to unshare an item from this organization, or
   - unshare an item from a different organization (you are a member of) by [changing your organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) before continuing on.
3. <a id="locating-your-item-for-unsharing" />[Locate](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#finding-secure-content) the personally owned file (which [you](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-and-storing-personally-owned-content) or a [collaborator](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-content-to-share-through-another-users-folder) had previously uploaded) or folder to unshare.
4. Do one of the following to begin unsharing this item:

   - To remove *all* collaborators from this item, either:

     - Right-click on the item's row and choose **Unshare** from the pop-up menu:

       ![Right-clicking a shared file as an owner - context menu](/images/f5240ffe0480-right-clicking-a-shared-file-context-menu-owner.png)

       <br />
       *OR*

     1. *Select* the item, by clicking on the item's row (to the right of its name if a folder): <br />
        ![Selecting a shared item as an owner](/images/46df31113312-selecting-a-shared-item-owner.png)
     2. In the toolbar area near the top of the page, click the **More** drop-down and choose **Unshare**.
   - To remove *specific* collaborators from this item, either:

     1. *Select* the item.
     2. In the **Sharing and Permissions** section of the **Details** tab on the right, locate the appropriate collaborators' email addresses and from their respective **Permission** drop-downs, choose **Remove user**.

     *OR*

     1. Right-click on the item's row and choose **Share** from the pop-up menu.
     2. On the **Share** dialog box, click the **Remove** link associated with the collaborator/s to remove from this item.
     3. Click **Share** to record your modifications on this dialog box.

## Renaming an item

An item can be renamed if:

- you own the item or
- a Covata user has [shared](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) the item with you (as a collaborator) with **Manage** [permissions](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-a-permission-set) on the item.

**To rename an item:**

1. [Sign in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. If the Home icon at the top-left indicates the word 'Home' and no drop-down icon is indicated, skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-your-item-for-renaming). (This indicates that you are a member of only one organization.) <br />
   *Otherwise*, the name of your current organization is indicated, from which you can either:

   - skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-your-item-for-renaming) to rename an item from this organization, or
   - rename an item from a different organization (you are a member of) by [changing your organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) before continuing on.
3. <a id="locating-your-item-for-renaming" />[Locate](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#finding-secure-content) the item to rename.
4. Do one of the following to begin renaming this item:

   - Right-click on the item's row and choose **Rename** from the pop-up menu:

     ![Right-clicking a file as an owner - context menu](/images/fdb17eed7ea6-right-clicking-a-file-context-menu-owner.png)

     <br />
     **Note:** If the **Rename** menu option is missing, then it is not possible to rename this item as its owner did not grant you **Manage** permissions when they [shared the item (or one of its ancestral folders)](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with you. <br />
     *OR*

   1. *Select* the item, by clicking on the item's row (to the right of its name if a folder): <br />
      ![Selecting an item as its owner](/images/c4250f7233ae-selecting-an-item-owner.png)
   2. In the toolbar area near the top of the page, click the **More** drop-down and choose **Rename**. Note that this option will not be available if the owner did not grant **Manage** permissions when they [shared the item (or one of its ancestral folders)](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with you.
5. In the **Rename File/Folder** dialog box, specify a new name for the item. <br />
   **Note:** If the item is a file, it is not possible to change the file's extension. It is only possible to alter a file name extension with the Safe Share web application before the file is uploaded to the application. Note that [Safe Share for Windows](/3.0/windows/index) may [be used to alter file extensions](/3.0/windows/managing-secure-content#renaming-an-item) after files have been uploaded. <br />
6. Click **Save** to complete renaming the item.

## Moving an item

Files and folders (i.e. items) can be moved from one location to another, all of which are accessible from the items area of the main Safe Share page (within your organization).

As a Covata user, you can move:

- An item that you own to:

  - another folder you own (or the root location of the items area) or
  - a folder owned (and [shared](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with you) by another Covata user who granted you **Manage** [permissions](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-a-permission-set) on this folder.
- An item contained within a folder owned (and shared with you) by another Covata user (who granted you **Manage** permissions on this folder) to any subfolder of this folder. You require **Manage** permissions on the item and the target folder to which you are moving the item - i.e. the owner must not have reduced your permissions on the item you are moving and the target folder. <br />
  **Note:** Although a Covata user may have granted you **Manage** permissions on two separate folder hierarchies, it is not possible to move an item from one of these folder hierarchies to the other.

## Removing personally owned content

Removing personally owned secure content which [you](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-and-storing-personally-owned-content) or a [collaborator](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-content-to-share-through-another-users-folder) had previously uploaded (using any Safe Share application), both:

- Removes this content from storage managed by the Covata Platform and also
- Prevents other people you have shared this content with from being able to access it.

**To remove personally owned secure content:**

1. [Sign in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. If the Home icon at the top-left indicates the word 'Home' and no drop-down icon is indicated, skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-your-item-to-remove). (This indicates that you are a member of only one organization.) <br />
   *Otherwise*, the name of your current organization is indicated, from which you can either:

   - skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-your-item-to-remove) to remove an item from this organization, or
   - remove an item from a different organization (you are a member of) by [changing your organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) before continuing on.
3. <a id="locating-your-item-to-remove" />[Locate](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#finding-secure-content) the personally owned file or folder (which [you](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-and-storing-personally-owned-content) or a [collaborator](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-content-to-share-through-another-users-folder) had previously uploaded/created). <br />
   **Tip:** To ensure you are only working with your own items in the list of items area, click the **All files** drop-down at the top-left of the main Safe Share page and choose the **My files** [filter](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#filtering-items).
4. Do one of the following to begin removing this item:

   - Right-click on the item's row and choose **Remove** from the pop-up menu:

     ![Right-clicking a file as an owner - context menu](/images/fdb17eed7ea6-right-clicking-a-file-context-menu-owner.png)

     <br />
     *OR*

   1. *Select* the item, by clicking on the item's row (to the right of its name if a folder): <br />
      ![Selecting an item as its owner](/images/c4250f7233ae-selecting-an-item-owner.png)
   2. In the toolbar area near the top of the page, click the 'Remove' icon
      ![Remove icon](/images/8b16f9b754af-remove-icon.png)

       in the toolbar area near the top of the page.

   The Safe Share web application prompts you to confirm this removal and upon clicking **Yes**, your item and its content is removed from storage. Any people you shared this item's content with will no longer be able to access the item.

## Removing content shared through another user's folder

Removing secure content that you have **Manage** [permissions](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#selecting-a-permission-set) on, which either you or someone else had [previously uploaded to another user's folder](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-content-to-share-through-another-users-folder) or created (through any Safe Share application), both:

- Removes this content from storage managed by the Covata Platform and also
- Prevents other people who were collaborators on this content from being able to access it.

**To remove secure content which you or someone else had uploaded to another user's folder through Safe Share:**

1. [Sign in](/3.0/user/signing-in) to the Safe Share web application.
2. If the Home icon at the top-left indicates the word 'Home' and no drop-down icon is indicated, skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-another-users-item-to-remove). (This indicates that you are a member of only one organization.) <br />
   *Otherwise*, the name of your current organization is indicated, from which you can either:

   - skip to the [next step](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#locating-another-users-item-to-remove) to remove an item from this organization, or
   - remove an item from a different organization (you are a member of) by [changing your organization](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#changing-organisations) before continuing on.
3. <a id="locating-another-users-item-to-remove" />[Locate](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#finding-secure-content) the file (which you or someone else had [previously uploaded to another user's folder](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#uploading-content-to-share-through-another-users-folder)) or subfolder ([created within another user's folder](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#organising-content-with-folders)) to be removed. <br />
   **Tip:** To ensure you are only working with items that other Covata users have shared with you, click the **All files** drop-down at the top-left of the main Safe Share page and choose the **Shared with me** [filter](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#filtering-items) on the left of the main Safe Share page to show only items (i.e. including folders) that other users have shared with you.
4. Do one of the following to begin removing this item:

   - Right-click on the item's row and choose **Remove** from the pop-up menu:

     ![Right-clicking a file as a collaborator with Manage permissions - context menu](/images/38da27f08215-right-clicking-a-file-context-menu-collaborator-manage.png)

     <br />
     **Note:** If the **Move** and **Rename** menu options are missing (and **Access** within the **File/Folder Details** section of the **Details** tab does not indicate the **Manage** permissions icon), then the owner of this item did not grant you **Manage** permissions when they [shared the item (or one of its ancestral folders)](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with you. Therefore, clicking **Remove** in this situation will [remove your access to this content](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#removing-your-access-to-secure-content-shared-with-you). <br />
     *OR*

   1. *Select* the item, by clicking on the item's row (to the right of its name if a folder): <br />
      ![Selecting an item as a collaborator with Manage permissions](/images/83f9a2054eaa-selecting-an-item-collaborator-manage.png)
   2. In the toolbar above the list of items, click the **Remove** button. Note that if the **More** menu (with its **Move** and **Rename** options) is missing and **Access** within the **File/Folder Details** section of the **Details** tab does not indicate the **Manage** permissions icon, then the owner of this item did not grant you **Manage** permissions when they [shared the item (or one of its ancestral folders)](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with you. Therefore, clicking **Remove** in this situation will [remove your access to this content](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#removing-your-access-to-secure-content-shared-with-you).

   The Safe Share web application prompts you to confirm this removal and upon clicking **Yes**, the item and its content is removed from storage. Any people who had access to this item's content will no longer have access to it.

      ![Selecting an item as a collaborator](/images/56fa093bcef4-selecting-an-item-collaborator.png)
   2. In the toolbar above the list of items, click the **Remove** button. Note that if the **More** menu (with its **Move** and **Rename** options) is present and **Access** within the **File/Folder Details** section of the **Details** tab indicates the **Manage** permissions icon, then the owner of this item granted you **Manage** permissions when they [shared the item (or one of its ancestral folders)](/3.0/user/uploading-storing-and-sharing-content-securely#sharing-secure-content) with you. Therefore, clicking **Remove** in this situation will instead [remove this content](/3.0/user/managing-secure-content#removing-content-shared-through-another-users-folder).

   The Safe Share web application prompts you to confirm this removal and upon clicking **Yes**, your access to this item is removed and the item is removed from your list of items area.
