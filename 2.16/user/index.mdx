## Overview

Safe Share is a web application (of the Covata Platform) which allows you to securely share and access any data anywhere.

Using Safe Share, you can securely upload, store, download and share large files easily through a web browser on your computer.

Since Safe Share is a web application, it can be accessed from any location - even publicly beyond an organization's private network, thereby allowing files to be shared securely between an organization's people (especially those on the move) as well as external people, such as customers and partners.

All uploaded files are encrypted before they reach storage managed by the Covata Platform. The owners of these encrypted files can share these files with other people within their organization, including any other external people who may not yet have been registered on the Covata Platform.

The Safe Share web application can be accessed conveniently from your desktop/laptop computer, a tablet or a phone or any other device with a compatible web browser.
